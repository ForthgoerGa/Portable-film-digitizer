## Objectives
- Schematic design: Motor drivers and Power Management.

## Work Completed
- Switched from servos to two NEMA 17 stepper motors for the film advancement mechanism. Servos lacked the precision needed for 4K alignment.

- Integrated the A4988 stepper driver into the schematic.

- Added 100uF decoupling capacitors to the VMOT pins of the A4988 to handle current spikes.

## Design Decisions and Engineering Process
- Reasoning for NEMA 17: Holding torque is required to keep the film flat against the light chamber. NEMA 17 provides $40+ N\cdot cm$ of torque compared to the limited precision of micro-servos.
