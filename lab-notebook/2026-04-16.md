# 2026-04-16

## Objectives
- Integration with Raspberry Pi and HAT+ Specification check.

## Work Completed
- Verified the PCB dimensions against the Raspberry Pi HAT+ Specification.

- Integrated the ID_EEPROM to allow the Pi to auto-configure the GPIOs for the AXI interface.

- Issue: The 5V rail from the Pi was sagging to 4.7V during motor acceleration.

- Fix: Added a 470uF bulk capacitor to the 5V input rail on the PCB.

## Measurements and Results
- Voltage ripple on 3.3V rail during motor stall: < 50mV.

- Digitization speed: Average of 1.2 seconds per frame (Hardware latency only).
