## Objectives
- Stepper motor logic implementation and testing.

## Work Completed
- Wrote the driver for A4988 using the STM32 HAL.
- Implemented microstepping (1/16) to ensure smooth film advancement.
- Calculations: - Steps per revolution = $200 \times 16 = 3200$.
  - Desired linear resolution = 0.01mm. With a 10mm radius spindle, $C = 62.83mm$.
  - Resolution = $62.83 / 3200 \approx 0.019mm/step$. Close enough to target.

## Debugging Notes
- Motor was skipping steps at high speed.
- Action: Increased the current limit on the A4988 potentiometer to 0.8A (Measured $V_{ref} = 0.4V$). Skipped steps eliminated.
