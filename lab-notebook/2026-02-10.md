## High-Level Requirements (HLRs)
- 1. Resolution & Quality: The system must be capable of digitizing various film formats (35mm, 120 medium format, and Instax), outputting images with a minimum resolution of 4K (3840x2160). It must possess sufficient dynamic range to recover shadow and highlight details from the negatives. (Design Basis: Utilizing the Pi HQ Camera + 25mm lens + extension tube macro solution + stitching scan technology.)
- 2. Portability & Autonomy: The system must be a self-contained, portable device with physical dimensions limited to 30cm x 20cm x 15cm and a weight of less than 1.5kg. It must be powered by a lithium-ion battery, capable of scanning at least 10 rolls of film (360 exposures) on a single charge.
- 3. Speed & Automation: The system must implement automated lighting adjustment, film transport, and image capture workflows. The total duration from the moment the user presses "Capture" to receiving the processed digital image (via Wi-Fi upload and processing) must not exceed 15 seconds.

## Subsystem Requirements
### A. Power Supply
- **Requirement:** The Power Management Unit (PMU) must convert the 3S Li-Po battery (11.1V) input into three physically isolated voltage rails:
  - **5.0V (≥ 3A):** Stable logic supply for the Raspberry Pi and STM32.
  - **6.0V (≥ 3A):** Dedicated high-current rail for driving servo motors.
  - **12V (Constant Voltage):** For the LED illumination system.
- **Target:** During motor stalls or high-load events, logic circuit voltage fluctuations must not exceed **±5%**, preventing system resets.

### B. Control Unit
- **Requirement:** The control system must employ a **Master–Slave Dual-Core Architecture**:
  - **STM32 (MCU):** Acts as the low-level hardware controller, responsible for reading user inputs and generating PWM signals for precise motor positioning and LED brightness control.
  - **Raspberry Pi (SBC):**  Acts as the high-level co-processor, communicating with the STM32 via USB/UART. It is responsible for driving the HQ Camera for image acquisition and executing data uploads via Wi-Fi.
- **Target:** This architecture fulfills the course’s assessment requirements regarding “low-level hardware design skills.”

### C. Imaging & Illumination
- **Requirement:** The system must integrate an **IMX477 12MP sensor** (paired with a 25mm lens and 10mm extension tubes for macro focusing) and a **dual-mode LED array** (high-CRI backlight for negatives and reflective light for photo paper). The system must automatically switch light sources based on media type and provide uniform, flicker-free illumination.

### D. Motion System
- **Requirement:** The motion system must utilize multiple **Hitec HS-318 standard servos**, providing at least **3.7 kgf·cm** of stall torque at **6.0V**. These will drive the film carrier or sensor platform for **sub-millimeter precision positioning** on the X–Y axes to support multi-frame stitching and automatic film winding.
