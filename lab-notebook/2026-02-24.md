## Objectives
- Component selection and footprint verification.

## Work Completed
- TA Meeting Notes: TA suggested checking the current rating for the LED backlight. If using high-CRI LEDs, the peak current might exceed 500mA.
- Selected the AMS1117-3.3 for the logic rail and a switching regulator for the 5V/12V rails to maintain efficiency.
- Calculated logic power: $P_{logic} \approx (150mA \times 3.3V) = 495mW$.

## Measurements and Results
- Thermal check for AMS1117: With $V_{in} = 5V$, $\Delta V = 1.7V$. $P_d = 1.7V \times 0.15A = 0.255W$. According to the datasheet, $R_{\theta JA}$ is approx $60^{\circ}C/W$ (with 1000 $mm^2$ copper), resulting in a $15.3^{\circ}C$ rise. This is acceptable without a dedicated heatsink.

## Issues and Risks
- Potential EMI from the NEMA 17 stepper motors interfering with the high-CRI LED PWM signals.
